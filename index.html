1 · Edit image
Tone

Band finding expects dark bands on a light background. Invert white-on-black images before reading the results.

Geometry

The pictures show what each end of a slider does to the image (dashed: before). Double-click a slider to reset it.

The rectangle is what's analysed. On loading, a photo with plain background round the gel (a light box, scanner bed or bench) starts cropped to the gel; otherwise it's the whole image. Blank corners from rotating contribute nothing. Drag the rectangle's corners and edges to change it (e.g. to leave out labels above the lanes).

2 · Lanes and bands

Lanes

Find lanes fits evenly spaced lanes to where the bands are (empty lanes between loaded ones count; empty edges start as empty space); straighten the gel first, as tilted lanes blur the gaps. Above the gel, clicking a lane's number steps it through: a lane → empty (∅: nothing loaded; it keeps its number, is hatched, and is left off the figure unless "annotate empty lanes" is ticked) → removed (×: space between lanes) → a lane again (+). On DNA gels empty lanes are found for you. Typing a number places exactly that many. Drag the dashed dividers to adjust.

Ladders

Ladder bands are the filled circles, labelled with their size. If one is on the wrong band, drag it to the right one; masses update as you go.

Band finding

Edited image: the picture you see. Original pixels: each pixel of the loaded image is placed through the edits and counted once, never blended.

Lane annotations

Lane labels are used above the lanes on the figure; annotations and volumes aren't. Paste several lines into a box to fill that lane and the ones below (columns from a spreadsheet fill across). Groups (e.g. non-reducing / reducing): add a column, name it, then click a lane's cell and drag to cover a continuous run of lanes. Click a lone selected cell to clear it.

Sample preparation new

Type each lane's concentration and the amount to load: the sample volume is amount ÷ concentration, made up to the total with the diluent, loading dye and, in lanes ticked Reduced, the reducing agent (lanes in a group named Reduced start ticked). Leave the amount blank to use as much sample as fits. The amounts go in the caption.

Band annotations

Each band you've marked, with its estimated size ± uncertainty, read off the standard curve (log size against distance down the gel) at the band's own row. Each lane's curve is the ladder's bands carried straight across to it; with a ladder on each side, along the line joining each size in the two, so a gel that ran a little faster on one side is followed. The ± combines the band's position, its own width, the ladder bands' width and the curve's own uncertainty. A little past either end of the ladder (up to 1.5x its end size), sizes are extrapolated in a straight line, shown with "~" and a ± that grows with the distance; further out, ">" / "<" the end size. Show profiles to see the curves with your bands on them.
