1 · Edit image
Tone

Band finding expects dark bands on a light background. Invert white-on-black images before reading the results.

Geometry

Skew slides rows sideways (straightens leaning lanes). Distort slides columns up and down (levels bands that slope across the gel). Perspective narrows the top or bottom. Double-click a slider to reset it.

Crop

Drag the rectangle or its corners and edges. It can't include any area outside the original image.
